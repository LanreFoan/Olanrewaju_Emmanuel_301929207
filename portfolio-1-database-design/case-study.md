# Case Study Statement - MetriCare Specialist Hospital (simulated)

**Organisation/scenario:** MetriCare Specialist, a simulated private multi-department outpatient clinic.

**Nature of the system:** An outpatient clinic management system covering patient registration, appointment booking, consultations, diagnoses, laboratory, radiology, pharmacy & inventory, billing, reports & analytics.

**Users of the database:**

| User | Needs |
|---|---|
| Front-desk staff | Register patients, schedule or cancel appointments |
| Billing officers | Generate invoices, record payments, manage outstanding balances |
| Physicians | View patient history, log diagnoses, prescribe medications, order lab tests |
| Nurses | Update real-time vitals, log administered medication, track ward bed |
| Pharmacists | Verify & dispense prescriptions, manage stock |
| Lab scientist | Receive test orders, record test results |
| Radiologist | Upload and attach medical images to patients' profiles |
| Management | Workload, revenue and disease-trend reports |

**Major data stored:** patients, doctors and departments, appointments, consultations, diagnoses, medications and stock, prescriptions, lab tests/orders/results, invoices and payments.

**Key operations supported:**
1. Book an appointment without duplicating booking a doctor
2. Retrieve a patient's full history
3. Record consultation, diagnosis, prescription, and lab orders/results
4. Dispense medication 
5. Invoice and record payments
6. Management reporting (workload, top diagnoses, outstanding balances)


*All data is synthetic. No real patient data is used.*
